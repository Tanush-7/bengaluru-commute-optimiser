What this tool does not know.

Not live data

Times come from typical peak and off-peak speeds, not live traffic. Cab fares are a modelled estimate — the app quote on the day can be higher or lower, and surge changes everything.

Metro coverage

The picker covers the operational Purple and Green lines, routed via Majestic; rail distances and the fare that comes from them are approximate until you overwrite the fare field. The Yellow line (RV Road–Bommasandra, for Electronic City) opened in 2025 and is not in the picker yet — enter your fare manually if you use it. Metro only wins if both ends are genuinely near a station.

Fare sources

Auto: RTA meter fixed July 2025 (₹36 first 2 km, ₹18/km), still the official rate as of Nov 2026 — app autos charge a premium over it. Metro: distance-slab estimate (₹10–₹90, unofficial chart; BMRCL confirms a 5% smart-card discount but publishes no English slab table) — editable, so use your real fare.
